## ✨ Highlights

- I went to see Nilüfer Yanya with my friend Rachael, she’s really good!
- I managed to get my ADHD care transferred from a private clinic to the NHS 🎉
- Went to a fun Christmas comedy evening and to drag bingo with my friends.
- My mum came to stay with me over Christmas 🎄
- And then we went down to my sister's in Devon to celebrate her birthday 🎈

## 🎮 Played/Read/Watched

- [Shrinking](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt15677150/) season 2: fun, feel good TV.
- [Four Thousand Weeks](https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/four-thousand-weeks-embrace-your-limits-change-your-life-make-your-four-thousand-weeks-count-oliver-burkeman/534030ff5e54bc10?ean=9781784704001) by Oliver Burkeman: this book is SO GOOD. I cannot recommend it enough. As someone that got tired of feeling perpetually behind in life and started to think about what I want to do, this was a really great reset.
- [Hamnet](https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/hamnet-maggie-o-farrell/dd919bf676726a03?ean=9781472223821&bkshp-astro=t) by Maggie O’Farrel: so well written, and so sad 😭
- [Abbott Elementary](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt14218830/) S4: still fun!
- [Sanctuary](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/sanctuary-2022/) - Margaret Qualley can do no wrong, swear to god.
- [The Substance](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/the-substance/) - it was fun and weird and different, i liked it
- [Couples Therapy](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt10665386/) series 4: Orna does it again!
- [The Discreet Charm of the Bourgeoisie](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/the-discreet-charm-of-the-bourgeoisie/): I liked it, but didn’t love it, tbh.
- [Alone Star State: Wim Wenders on the deep impact of Paris, Texas at 40](https://letterboxd.com/journal/paris-texas-wim-wenders-interview-deep-impact/)
- [The Day of the Beast](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/the-day-of-the-beast/): This was a fun watch, but not remarkable.
- [It's Lonely at the Centre of the Earth](https://app.notion.com/p/Lifenotes-49-December-2024-3150c22fd030805c865cd9b094412b1e?pvs=21) by Zoe Thorogood: this comic is excellent! She does such a good job of communicating emotions through the format and by breaking it, really worth a read.
- [Memories of Murder](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/memories-of-murder/): I loved this one a lot, a great story, and a surprising number of flying kicks.
- [James Acaster: Hecklers Welcome](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt34581142/)
- [Work Won't Love You Back](https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/work-won-t-love-you-back-how-devotion-to-our-jobs-keeps-us-exploited-exhausted-and-alone-sarah-jaffe/7574757b5543f98d?ean=9781787386822&bkshp-astro=t) by Sarah Jaffe: I started listening to the audiobook of this one and while it was making great points, I was finding it really depressing. So I didn't finish it.
- [When Harry Met Sally](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/when-harry-met-sally/): I've never seen this one before and it was pretty good. I can see why people like it.
- [Ghost in the Shell](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/ghost-in-the-shell/): This was really great. Watching it now, it’s so clear how much it inspired over the years.
- [Ethical Web Principles - W3C Statement](https://www.w3.org/TR/ethical-web-principles/)
- [My Personal Experience with RSI - Josh Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/blog/mindbody/)
- [Ali Wong: Single Lady](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt33350315/)
- [Basic Sanitation, the Movie](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/basic-sanitation-the-movie/): I watched this with my mum and it was really, really fun. A lot of great actors doing a really good comedy.
- [Chinese Take-Away](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/chinese-take-away/): My mum really likes this one. It's a nice feel good story and it stars Ricardo Darín.
- [Rock in Rio: The History](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt21563862/): My mum recommended me this one, so we watched it together.
- [Little Miss Sunshine](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/little-miss-sunshine/): Another rec from my mom. It was nice.
- [The Heist of the Century](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/the-heist-of-the-century/): This is really good. The heist was so clever, made it a really fun watch.
- [In Bruges](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/in-bruges/): Those two are so good together!!
- [The Weasel’s Tale](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/the-weasels-tale/): It was fine.
- [Casual Viewing by Will Tavlin](https://www.nplusonemag.com/issue-49/essays/casual-viewing/)
- [Underground](https://letterboxd.com/carolgilabert/film/underground-1995/): This is one of my favorite movies ever. It is so, so good. I've been watching it with my family since I was a kid and it was really nice to watch it again and see it holds up.
