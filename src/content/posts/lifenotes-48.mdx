## ✨ Highlights

- I went to Berlin for a weekend with my friend Mark. It was really fun to just mooch around, have nice food and hang out with him.
- I went to see Ezra Collective with my friends Nisha and Rachael. It was SO GOOD and we danced so much 💃
- I went to see A Raisin in the Sun with my friend Rachel. It was a really emotional play, I really enjoyed it.
- I went on a hidden London tour with some of my work friends, to the unused tube station The Strand. It was very, very cool, I'd recommend it.
- My friend Hayden came to visit and we went to Sheffield to unpack his house that he sold 📦
- The survey on the house that I'm buying came up with some damp problems 👎
- But I went to view a different house on a road that I really like and I've put an offer on it 🎉

## 🎮 Played/Read/Watched

- [Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow by Gabrielle Zevin](https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/tomorrow-and-tomorrow-and-tomorrow-discover-the-moving-powerful-sunday-times-bestseller-that-everyone-is-talking-about-gabrielle-zevin/7312831?ean=9781529115543&next=t): This was a really fun read about video games, friendship and love.
- [Wings of desire](https://letterboxd.com/film/wings-of-desire/): It was such a lovely experience to watch this at the Babylon Cinema in Berlin. It was a really cozy space and a really lovely film.
- [Anora](https://letterboxd.com/film/anora/): I went to see this at the cinema with my friend Tom and it was a really fun watch.
- [Intermezzo by Sally Rooney](https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/intermezzo-sally-rooney/c192733f5377b061?ean=9780571365487&next=t): I enjoyed this one, but not as much as her previous books.
- [Unreal](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3314218/?ref_=nv_sr_srsg_0_tt_8_nm_0_in_0_q_unreal): This TV show is insane. All the characters are kinda horrible, but it is very, very watchable.
- [We read the paper that forced Timnit Gebru out of Google. Here’s what it says. by Karen Hao](https://www.technologyreview.com/2020/12/04/1013294/google-ai-ethics-research-paper-forced-out-timnit-gebru/)
- [Indika](https://store.playstation.com/en-gb/product/EP4361-PPSA16214_00-0000000000INDIKA): This was okay. I enjoy a puzzle based game and the setting was cool. But it wasn't amazing.
- [Deathloop](https://store.playstation.com/en-gb/product/EP1003-PPSA01670_00-DEATHLOOP0000000): This game was so much fun, so different to most games I've played before.
- [Staff Engineer: Leadership beyond the management track by Will Larson](https://staffeng.com/book/)
- [A quiet place: day one](https://letterboxd.com/film/a-quiet-place-day-one/): It was fine.
- [Control](https://store.playstation.com/en-gb/product/EP4040-CUSA11454_00-CONTROLUEBUNDLE0): This game is so good. Such a good atmosphere, and very fun to play. And the visuals look incredible.
