## ✨ Highlights

- Went on a work trip to Cardiff with my team. It's a really lovely city and had a fun time 😌
- The house sale seems to be moving 🤞🏼
- I went to Liverpool for a few days with my family, we hung out there for a few days and then they came to stay at mine for a bit too 💛

## 🎮 Played/Read/Watched

- [Cult Classic](https://bookshop.org/p/books/cult-classic-sloane-crosley/18721618), by Sloane Crosley: This was quite a weird book. It was a fun read, but largely unremarkable.
- [Star Wars Jedi: Survivor](https://store.playstation.com/en-gb/concept/10005114): I'd enjoyed the previous game in the series, so I decided to give this one a go, it’s also really good fun. It's very cathartic to slice through aliens with a lightsaber.
- I went to see a marathon of the Star Wars original trilogy at my local cinema - [Broadway](https://broadway.org.uk/). It was a fun, chill day.
- [Why GOV.UK’s Exit this Page component doesn’t use the Escape key by Beeps](https://beeps.website/blog/2024-10-09-why-govuk-exit-this-page-doesnt-use-escape/#bonus%3A-why-does-it-redirect-the-user-to-bbc-weather%3F) - This was an insightful read. I learnt a few bits about the web and about a great feature.
- [Why, after 6 years, I'm over GraphQL by Paul Bessey](https://bessey.dev/blog/2024/05/24/why-im-over-graphql/)
- [The Unfettered Selfishness of Digital Nomads by James Greig](https://www.thrillist.com/travel/nation/digital-nomads-criticism-locals-exploitation)
- [The Sympathizer](https://bookshop.org/p/books/the-sympathizer-a-novel-pulitzer-prize-for-fiction-associate-professor-of-english-and-american-studies-and-ethnicity-viet-thanh-nguyen/02cb916e891f0d4a) by Viet Thanh Nguyen: I really loved this book. I read it cover to cover pretty quickly, I'd highly recommend it.
